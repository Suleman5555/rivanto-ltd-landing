Common questions

Where do you deliver?

We serve customers in Germany and across Europe. Delivery options and times are shown at checkout — standard delivery €4.95, free in Germany on orders over €50.

How do I pay?

Payment is taken through the marketplace where you place your order, using its secure checkout. We do not take card payments directly on this website.
